Check eligibility before you order

Government support and Tesla sales promotions are different. Do not assume that an advertised vehicle discount means you qualify for a government grant.

  • Check whether the official programme is open and whether your vehicle and purchase type qualify.
  • Review any household-income requirements and the documents requested by the programme.
  • Check application timing before placing an order or registering the vehicle.
  • Confirm whether funding can be combined with other offers.

Check the official funding information. If the programme is unavailable or has changed, do not rely on an older summary.

Planning a Tesla order?

For referral instructions and offer details, see the English Tesla referral link guide or the German referral guide. Check the current benefit directly with Tesla before ordering. Sales promotions and referral rewards are separate.
